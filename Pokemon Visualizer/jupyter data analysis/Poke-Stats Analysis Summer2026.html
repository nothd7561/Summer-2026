Fighting and Dragon have the highest physical attack, along with Ground and Steel; this makes sense for very bulky and physical Pokémon. While types such as water, ghost, grass, and fairy are more all-rounded. Utilizing common sense, we can assume the statistics of Pokémon types are based on real-world assumptions and stereotypes assigned to the elements. This is just using the statistics; we can also factor in the number of Pokémon for each type later.¶
